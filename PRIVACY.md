# DyslexAid privacy policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

DyslexAid does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There
is nothing to opt out of because nothing is gathered.

## What the extension does with your data

The only data DyslexAid keeps is your own settings: which features are on,
your text size, your tint color, your bionic boldness, your ruler height,
your read-aloud speed, and the list of sites where you paused it. These are
saved with Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync`, which means they live in
your Chrome profile and, if you are signed into Chrome, sync between your
own devices through your Google account. DyslexAid never sees them leave
your browser and has no server of its own.

## What it does not do

- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
- No network requests of any kind
- No reading of page content for any purpose other than restyling it on
  screen or reading it aloud through your browser's own speech engine
- No accounts, sign-ins, or third-party services

Read aloud uses the speech synthesis built into Chrome. Text is spoken by
your computer and is not sent anywhere.

## Permissions, and why

- `storage`: saves your settings
- `activeTab` and host access on all sites: the extension's only job is
  restyling whatever page you are reading, so it has to run on the pages you
  open
- `scripting`: injects the styling script into pages

## Changes
